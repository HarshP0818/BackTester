# Emerging and Disruptive Technologies Journal Entry

## Part One

### Technology 1: Cloud Computing
- Identification and description: Cloud computing is a paradigm where computing resources such as servers, storage, networking, and software are delivered over the internet on demand. It disrupted traditional IT by moving infrastructure from local data centers to managed services from providers like AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud.
- Likely impacts on computer science or my career: Cloud computing has shifted the focus of computer science from owning hardware to designing scalable, resilient distributed systems. For my career, it means I need skills in cloud architecture, DevOps practices, automation, and security to build modern applications.
- Impact on humans, communities, or the world: Cloud computing democratizes access to powerful tools and services, enabling startups, schools, and non-profits to launch solutions without heavy capital investment. It supports global collaboration, remote work, and faster innovation in health, education, and business.

### Technology 2: Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
- Identification and description: Artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) refer to systems that learn patterns from data and make predictions or decisions. Recent advances in deep learning, natural language processing, and computer vision have made AI a major disruptive technology.
- Likely impacts on computer science or my career: AI/ML is changing core computer science problems by introducing data-driven design, model training, and ethical considerations. In my career, I will need knowledge of algorithms, data preprocessing, model evaluation, and how to integrate intelligent behavior into applications.
- Impact on humans, communities, or the world: AI/ML can improve healthcare diagnostics, personalize education, automate routine tasks, and optimize resource use. It also raises questions about bias, privacy, and job displacement, so communities need thoughtful policies and inclusive design.

### Combined Impact and Perspective
- How these technologies impact humans, communities, or the world: Together, cloud computing and AI/ML enable powerful services at scale, such as global data platforms, smart assistants, and real-time analytics. They make it possible for people everywhere to use advanced software without owning expensive equipment.
- Which course outcomes have I achieved so far, and which remain: I have achieved outcomes in software design and engineering, implementation of algorithms and data structures, and working with databases in a practical project. Remaining outcomes include deeper mastery of advanced optimization, deployment of cloud-native systems, and continuing to refine my ePortfolio artifacts with documented evidence of learning.
